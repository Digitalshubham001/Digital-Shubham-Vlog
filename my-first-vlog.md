1. Virtual Tours & 3D Walkthroughs

Most buyers now prefer exploring properties online even before visiting.
3D walkthroughs help people:

See interiors clearly

Check natural sunlight

Understand spacing and layout

Explore amenities virtually

This saves a lot of time and helps in quicker decision-making.

Developers now send everything instantly through WhatsApp:

Prices

Floor plans

EMI breakdowns

Offers

Chatbots and cloud portals make the process super fast.

3. Online Booking & Paperless Documentation

From EOI to final booking — everything can be done online now.
This boosts transparency and increases buyer confidence.
